A teacher from the people of the Quran

Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullah.

I am Abdul Baseer Anwar, an Alim graduate of Darul Uloom Hyderabad and a Hafiz of the Noble Quran. My studies covered the full Dars-e-Nizami curriculum — Arabic grammar and morphology, rhetoric and literature, Tafsir, Hadith, and Fiqh with its principles.

I teach students of every age and level — from a child reading their very first letters to adults studying the classical texts.

My aim is simple: that every student reads the Book of Allah correctly, understands its language, and lives by what they learn.

Frequently asked questions

Are the classes online?

Yes — classes are held online (Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp video), so you can learn from anywhere in the world.

Which language are classes taught in?

Urdu, Hindi and English — and Arabic for advanced students.

Is the first class free?

Yes. The first trial class is free, so we can assess your level and get to know each other.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the course and the number of classes per week. Please get in touch for details.

Can I choose my class timings?

Yes. We agree on timings that suit your time zone — India, the Gulf, the UK or North America.

Which madhhab is Fiqh taught according to?

Fiqh is taught according to the Hanafi madhhab, following the Darul Uloom tradition.
