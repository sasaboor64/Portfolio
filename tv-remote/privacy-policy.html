Remote Plus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Remote Plus is a remote control for smart TVs on your home network. It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not collect, sell or share personal data.

What the app stores, and where

The following is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage:

  • The name and local network address of the TV you choose.
  • A security certificate the app creates so your TV can recognise it after pairing.
  • If you use Sony IP control: the Pre-Shared Key you type in, and your TV's app list and app icons.
  • Your settings: which apps are on the remote, what the Menu button does, trackpad or D-pad.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

Who the app talks to

Only your TV, directly over your local Wi-Fi network. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. When you tap an app shortcut, the app asks your TV to open that app; your TV may then contact that app's own service, as it would with its physical remote.

Permissions

  • Network access and Wi-Fi state: to find TVs on your network and send them commands.
  • Vibration: for button-press feedback.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
